The journey

Seven weeks to end the old story. One to cross the threshold. Seven to write the new one.

Weeks 1–7

The Origin

You learn story through the mediums you already love (the movie, the book, the poetry, the spirit, the myth, the adventure and the return), practicing the seven arts of storytelling on the most precious story of all: yours.

We rewrite and deprogram the stories that wrote us, the ones we never chose. You step far enough outside your life to see it as a story that can be rewritten, and go on an adventure into the gold buried in it. It's been described as a lifetime of therapy in a few weeks. Every one of you will reach a moment at your kitchen table, writing, in tears at the story that was always there.

Week 8

The Threshold

A week to land it in your body, with your one-to-one threshold session with Zachary and your crew around you. The old story closes for good.

Weeks 9–15

The Metamyth

Then we go to the future. We map the pattern of everything you went through and forge the meaning of your life into a story so much bigger than you that others will pay to be part of it. Your wound becomes the dragon the world is facing. Your purpose becomes a quest. Your transformation becomes an offer. We're going to utterly transform how you see money, and the world that story built. You walk your true story all the way into the future you author, and come back with the brand, the pitch and the fundable story, embodying the person you're becoming.

And it was never only your story. By the end, every epic in the room weaves into one.

We're going to love you through your excuses, raise the stakes of what you thought was possible and surround you for fifteen weeks with the community of the future, as we teach you everything you need to become the storyteller of your own life and a worldbuilder taking on the biggest challenges on earth.
