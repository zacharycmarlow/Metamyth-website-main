The story

The oldest journey there is, walked together.

Somewhere along the way, most of us forgot we were living inside a story someone else wrote: about money, work, success, who gets to matter, and what kind of future is even possible.

So we find ourselves alone, over-explaining, playing small, waiting for permission, paying to be lectured on Zoom, or carrying a vision so enormous we can barely explain it to the people we love.

Yet the same ancient pattern running through every story that shaped humanity can be used, consciously, to change the stories shaping our lives.

Every great adventure begins with someone who can no longer live inside the world as it is. Something calls them forward. They leave the familiar, face the monsters, find the treasure, and return with something that changes the world they came from.

We've been telling that story for thousands of years. What happens when we realize we're the characters?

The Kinship Quest is a collective initiation into that ancient pattern, using the seven arts of storytelling on the most precious story of all: your own. We begin by finding the stories you inherited, the ones you've been living without realizing you had a choice. We follow the patterns through your life, uncover the meaning hiding in the hardest things you've lived, and dig up the gold that's been there all along.

Then we take that gold into the future. Your life becomes the origin of a quest. What you care about becomes a calling. Your gifts become something you can offer the world, and you learn to tell that story so people understand it, feel it, and start helping you make it real.

And the turn that makes it epic: it was never only your story.

Every story in the room begins weaving into the others. Strangers become kin. The guides change as much as anyone. The future you're imagining starts filling with people who have their own wild reasons to be there.

We're moving out of the age of the lonely venture and back into the age of adventure. Out of the isolated, exhausted, heroic me, and into the unpredictable, world-changing we.

What starts as fifteen weeks of rewriting your life becomes a collective initiation into the timeline where we fucking did it. A community of the future, and you're invited to author it with us.

The journey

Seven weeks to end the old story. One to cross the threshold. Seven to write the new one.

Weeks 1–7

The Origin

You learn story through the mediums you already love (the movie, the book, the poetry, the spirit, the myth, the adventure and the return), practicing our seven arts of storytelling on the most precious story of all: yours.

We rewrite and deprogram the stories that wrote us, the ones we never chose. You step far enough outside your life to see it as a story that can be rewritten: the character you've been playing, the rules you inherited, the patterns that keep repeating, and the moments you thought were mistakes that turn out to hold the greatest meaning. Then you go on an adventure into the gold buried in it.

What happened stays what happened. What it means is yours to rewrite.

Every one of you will reach a moment at your kitchen table, writing, in tears at the story that was always there.

Week 8

The Threshold

A week to land it in your body, with your one-to-one threshold session with Zachary and your fellowship crew around you.

The threshold is where you step outside the old story for good and become who you've always been meant to be, while the past becomes your weapon to navigate the future.

Weeks 9–15

The Metamyth

Then we go to the future. We map the pattern of everything you went through and forge the meaning of your life into a story so much bigger than you that others will pay to be part of it. Your wound becomes the dragon the world is facing. Your purpose becomes a quest. Your transformation becomes an offer. Your vision becomes a future other people can see themselves inside.

We're going to utterly transform how you see money, and the world that story built. You'll learn to tell your story from origin to ask and make the invitation. We time travel into the future where you fucking did it, and you come back with the brand, the pitch and the fundable story, embodying the person you're becoming.

And by the end, every epic in the room weaves into one.

We're going to love you through your excuses, raise the stakes of what you thought was possible and surround you for fifteen weeks with the community of the future, as we teach you everything you need to become the storyteller of your own life and a worldbuilder taking on the biggest challenges on earth. And if you don't think that's you yet, chapter five is gonna fuck you up.

The room

The silence that changes everything.

The most powerful moments in this work have been moments of pure silence.

The kind that falls when someone has just stood in front of a room full of people and revealed something so honest, so strange, so beautiful, so completely themselves, that there's nothing left to say.

And the room loves them for it. Mess and all. Exactly as they are.

The things they thought were flaws become the things other people adore. The parts they thought were too much turn out to be the parts someone else needed to see.

When that happens again and again, the whole room changes. People stop trying so hard to be acceptable and start becoming more fully themselves. They see possibilities in one another that nobody could see alone. They start believing in futures they'd have been embarrassed to say out loud a few weeks earlier.

We collectively love each other into another world. And then we stand in awe of what just happened.
