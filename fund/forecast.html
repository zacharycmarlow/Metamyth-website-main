How it's built

  • We start from today: about $10K a month, one Adventure a month. Only offers we sell now are in the model: Kinship Quest cohorts, Adventures, enterprise work, Kinship membership, and the Origin app once it's built.
  • Kinship Quest runs three cohorts a year, in January, May and September: 14, 20 or 30 paid seats by case, about a third at $7,000 guided and the rest at $3,500 (the model prices guided seats at $6,500, so these figures run slightly conservative), growing a few percent each cohort. Half of each cohort is paid the month before it starts.
  • I take Adventures from one a month now to three, five or seven by month 9, at $8,000 or $10,000. Guides trained from our graduates arrive from mid to late 2027 and each leads two journeys a month at $6,000, keeping 30%.
  • Enterprise work starts at $21,000 in year one and $30,000 after. 50% to 85% of graduates join Kinship membership at about $325 a month. The app launches between October 2027 and April 2028 at $30 a month.
  • Costs carry the full team from day one, two more rounds of hires as revenue passes $60K and $100K a month, raises that follow revenue, 15% contingency, delivery costs by offer, and the $65K tech build. My pay is a $4K base, $6K from month 13, replaced by 40% of the Adventures I deliver once that's larger. 20% of profit goes to the team's profit share and 30% of the rest to a tax reserve.
  • The Mission Note is repaid from free cash flow after an 18-month build, once the business is profitable overall and keeps $50K in the bank: 40% of it, 50% once revenue passes $100K a month, at a multiple that rises from 1.2× to 2.5× the longer repayment takes.
